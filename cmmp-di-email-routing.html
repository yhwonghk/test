  1. IMX need to configure the routing table to send emails targeting the registered SMTP domains of the B/D directly to CMMP-DI, bypassing GCN

  4. GCN need move all registered SMTP email domains of the B/D to a Foreign Domain List, ensuring that emails routed via GCN to the migrated B/D are directed to CMMP-DI instead of the B/D’s Domino server
